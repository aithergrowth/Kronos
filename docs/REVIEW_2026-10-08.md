# The 8 October review: how strong the set is, what was wrong, what is open

Max: "ga een onderzoek doen naar al onze huidige structuur en alles wat we hebben, zijn er nog verbeterpunten, hoe is
onze setup nu, is deze sterk, hoe kijk jij ernaar?" Three independent read-only reviews of the code (the strategy
engine against Dorus's method, the live path against FTMO's rules, the backtester and the research process) plus the
numbers below. Everything found is either fixed in commit `a646f53` or listed under "Open" with a proposal.

## 1. What was wrong, and is fixed

| Defect | Where | Effect | Measured |
|---|---|---|---|
| The bias gate returned on the first direction's veto or missing required timeframe and never looked at the other direction | `strategy/bias.py` `combine_biases` | With `min_matching_timeframes: 2` (EURUSD) a bullish month and 4H under the weekly veto hid a bearish W+D+1H: shorts refused | No 2026 EURUSD trade changed; the state did not occur in a tradable moment |
| The 24 h touch rule counted from the zone candle's open | `strategy/engine.py` (`touch_age_hours`) | A daily zone had no hours, a 4H zone 20: no daily zone ever traded in 2024-2026 under the rule | With the clock right gold took two daily trades in 2024-2026 (134 trades, +82.6R against 132, +88.3R raw; the 2026 one -1R on a $211 stop, pushing out a +5.7R 1H trade); EURUSD unchanged (54, +38.4R). Daily zones are now off in the four profiles (`poi_timeframes: [4H, 1H]`), which is what actually ran |
| The confirmation search started at the candle after a touch seen on a finer timeframe | `strategy/confirmation.py` `find_confirmation` | The touch candle's sweep was left out of the stop on 4H and 1D zones | No 2026 trade changed on EURUSD, gold or NAS100 |
| `planned_risk` returned 0.0 when the terminal did not answer | `live.py` | The guard's worst case ran without the new trade's risk while the order went through | Now `None` and the entry is refused |
| The account lock counted a file stale after 60 s | `live.py` `AccountLock` | A slow terminal's own entry could outlive the lock and let a second window past the account's checks | Five minutes |
| Telegram error texts carried the bot token (the URL in `requests` errors) | `notify/telegram.py` | The token on the console | Redacted |
| A position without a stop counted as no risk and was named only in the 22:05 report | `live.py` `manage_positions`, `_can_open` | A stopless position on FTMO is unpriced exposure | Said on Telegram at once; no new entry until every position has a stop |

Checked and found correct: no look-ahead anywhere (views cut at close time, swings confirmed after `swing_right`,
monthly closes on the session calendar); the fill engine is conservative (stop and target in one candle -> the stop;
gaps -> the stop at the open; a limit filled and stopped in one candle -> a loss; bid/ask and commission counted once);
the guard's arithmetic with the stake multipliers (3 % open + 1.5 % -> refused; two 4H trades -> refused; resting
limits counted); sizing (lots floor to the step, below the minimum -> refused); the daily baseline (fails closed when
the history does not answer); the 23:00 server restart; stops travel in the order request.

These are in-sample numbers: the profiles were chosen on this window. The backtest review counted about 31
market-specific decisions dated 4-8 October (one per 5-16 trades of 2024-2026), about 250 variants screened, and the
in-sample mean rising from +0.28R to +0.63R a trade in 48 hours on the same 312 trades; the project's own holdout
("Chance" in the README) replicated at chance level. The same rules on June 2017 - 2023: EURUSD 0.00R, gold -0.10R,
NAS100 +0.37R, BTC -0.21R a trade. Its estimate of the forward edge: +0.0 to +0.2R a trade (central +0.1R), i.e.
15-30 % of the backtest's +0.63R; at 9.8 trades a month and 1.5 % that is about +1 to +2.5 % a month, 35-45 % losing
months, and a 10 % loss inside a challenge in roughly a third of starts rather than 1-4 %. My own stress test agrees in
direction: the actual 2024-2026 sequence at 1.5 % with the multipliers gives +9.8 % a month and 4 losing months of 32;
every trade 0.15R worse +7.9 % and 7 losing months; winners' R halved -9 % and the guard freezes the account. A year
drawn from the 32 months (block bootstrap, 4,000 paths): as backtested P(year < 0) 0 %, 0.15R worse 3 % (an 8 %
drawdown in 5 % of years), winners halved 21 % (an 8 % drawdown in 20 %).

Beyond the stake rules, the biggest unmodelled gap is the stop: it sits on the exact 5m/15m sweep extreme of the
HistData bid, and 15-21 % of the winners came within $0.5 / 3 pips of it before winning. FTMO's wicks differ in
a direction the backtest cannot see. An offset of a spread's width (`risk.sl_offset_pips`: EURUSD 1 pip, gold $0.30)
costs what it can see, 2024-2026: EURUSD 53 trades +32.8R against +38.4R, gold 133 trades +78.1R against +82.6R,
about 0.05-0.1R a trade. Not taken; the live record (fills against the planned stop in `ftmo_report.bat`) decides.

## 4. Open: decisions for Max

1. **A 4H-zone trade risks 3 % on NAS100 and gold and is held through the daily break, scheduled news and the
   weekend.** The guard prices every stop at its stop; a 100-point Monday gap on NAS100 (0.5 %) on a 7.5-lot position
   is -7.5 % in a day -> challenge failed. Options: the 4H multiplier 1.0 on NAS100 and gold (1.5 % a trade), or
   `--weekend-close` during the challenge and no 4H entry on a Friday afternoon. EURUSD and BTC are not exposed this way.
2. **Read the EURUSD bias from the dollar index**, as Dorus did on two of his three documented EURUSD trades (the
   bot refused both: on EURUSD's own levels the month, week and day read 50/50). The 12-trade DXY test of September
   said nothing. Building it is a day's work with no promise.
3. **Pin the code during a challenge**: `start_ftmo.bat` pulls the branch at every start, so a pushed commit goes
   live untested that evening. A tag per challenge, or leave as is and keep the rule that only tested changes are pushed.
4. **Positions outside the bot's magic number, or without a stop, are invisible to the guard** (counted as zero).
   Rule: no manual trades on the account; a live alert for a stopless position is a small addition.
5. The news calendar's seed file ends 2026-11-04; after that the ForexFactory feed alone carries the blackout.
6. The engine's `--costs ftmo` profile is below the measured FTMO costs (the research re-costs every ledger with the
   measured ones, so the quoted numbers are right; the engine's own should match).

## 5. Verdict

The code is a faithful, careful mechanisation of the method: no look-ahead, conservative fills, a guard that
counts the worst case, stops on the server. Three logic slips and three fail-open spots are fixed today; none of them
changed a 2026 trade on EURUSD, NAS100 or BTC.

The set is strong on 2024-2026 and that is also its weakness: it was chosen on those years, after many variants,
and the same rules lost in 2017-2023 on three of the four markets. The backtest's +0.63R a trade is the ceiling, not
the expectation; +0.1 to +0.2R is the honest central guess until the live record says more, and 20 live trades will
not say much. What holds up best: NAS100 (positive in both periods), gold's breadth (its R is not three trades), the
risk structure (two open, 1.5 %, the steps down, half on BTC and the 50/50 month). What to watch: BTC and EURUSD,
whose results rest on a handful of trades, and the 3 % 4H trades on NAS100 and gold over a gap.
